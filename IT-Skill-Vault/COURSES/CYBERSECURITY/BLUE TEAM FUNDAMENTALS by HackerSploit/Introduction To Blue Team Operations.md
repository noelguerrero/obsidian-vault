- Blue team operations consist of tools and techniques used by SOC team to defend and protect agains the attacks.

**The primary objective of the blue team are:**
- Incident response
- Network traffic analysis
- Analyzing logs and event correlation
- Threat modeling/intelligence
- Identifying threat actors and their C2 infrastucture
- Identifying suspicious activity and indicators of compromise.
- Digital forensics

**Threefold approach of Blue Team**
- Prevention (Hardening the organization security)
- Detection (Monitoring)
- Reactive Response (Incident Management)
