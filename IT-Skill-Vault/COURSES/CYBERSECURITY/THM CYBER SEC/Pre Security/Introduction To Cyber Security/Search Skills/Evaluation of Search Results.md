On the internet everyone can publish their on writings or opinion this can be through blogs, articles, or social media post. This ability makes possible for everyone to voice their claims even though there is no proof.

**It is our job, as readers, to evaluate the information. We will mention a few things to consider when evaluating information:**

-  **Source**: Identify the author or organization publishing the information. Consider whether they are reputable and authoritative on the subject matter. Publishing a blog post does not make one an authority on the subject. Identify the author or organization publishing the information. Verify if they are authoritative in that subject matter.

- **Evidence and reasoning**: Check if there claims have credible evidence and logical reasoning.

- **Objective and Bias:** Check if it is not bias (They can use it to promote or attack their rival).

- **Corroboration and consistency:** Check if multiple reliable sources are agree on that statement.
