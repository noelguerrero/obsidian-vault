## Network Types

- Small Home Networks - connect few computers and others to the internet.
- Small Office/Home Office (SOHO) Networks - allows home office or a remote office to connect to a corporate network and access resources. 
- Medium to Large Networks - (such as corporation or school) can have many location with hundred or thousands interconnected devices.
- World Wide Networks - it is a network of networks that connects million devices (internet).

## Data Transmission

### Types of Personal Data
- Volunteer data - data that you share voluntary
- Inferred data - data that generated by your activities (purchasing, location,  and so on)
- Observed data - your data that gets using an monitor feature like phone that track your location and smart watch that gets your heart beat and so on.

### The Bit
- It is the only language that computer understand. 
- 1 and 0 or on and off

A Bye consist of 8 bits that represent a certain characters
- **Capital letter:** A = 01000001
- **Number:** 9 = 00111001
- **Special character:** # = 00100011

### Common Methods of Data Transmission
After the data transformed into series of bits it convert it to signals to send it across the network through cables or radio waves

There are three common methods of signal transmission used in networks:
- **Electrical signals -** Transmission is achieved by representing data as electrical pulses on copper wire.
- **Optical signals -** Transmission is achieved by converting the electrical signals into light pulses.
- **Wireless signals -** Transmission is achieved by using infrared, microwave, or radio waves through the air.

## Bandwidth and Throughput

### Bandwidth
It is the maximum amount of data that can be transmitted over a communication channel in a given amount of time
- Kilobits per second (Kbps)
- Megabits per second (Mbps)
- Gigabits per second (Gbps)

### Throughput
- It is the actual amount of data that is successfully transmitted over the network in a given amount of time.
- Like bandwidth it measure like Kbps, Mbps, Gbps, and so on.

### Latency
- refers to the amount of time, including delays, for data to travel from one given point to another.
